FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know programming?

Not at all. Everything is done with clicks and menus. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can use parcellscope.

Can I really use it on my phone?

Yes. Every page is designed to work on phones, tablets, laptops and big screens, by day or in dark mode.

Who can use parcellscope?

Anyone with an account. Ask for one, then press Access App and sign in from any device.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It all runs in your web browser and is kept up to date for you.

I'm new to this. Where do I start?

The built-in guide walks you through every page, and each method is explained step by step in plain words.

Can I use the figures in my paper?

Yes. Every figure is ready to use in a paper, poster or presentation.
